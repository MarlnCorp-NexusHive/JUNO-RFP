JUNO RFP — Pursuit Lifecycle Capabilities

How a proposal team runs a bid from kickoff to submission

Audience: Proposal managers, capture leads, and reviewer / auditor teams · Shareable boilerplate

1. Ingest & understand

Drop the solicitation into Source Docs or Workspace. JUNO extracts text, tables, facts, and Q&A items so Section L/M requirements are not trapped in a 200-page PDF.

2. Position the pursuit

Company Intelligence profiles the issuer. Competitive Intelligence compares peer SIs on datasheet metrics, value propositions, and win themes — so the technical volume is not written in a vacuum.

3. Draft with reuse

Content Hub holds tagged boilerplate (including this pack). Writers ask document-grounded questions, then export a work document or slide deck instead of starting from a blank page.

4. Review like an evaluation board

Proposal Managers assign questions to RFP Auditors. Auditors draft (including AI assist), submit, and request clarifications. The PM reviews, requests changes, and keeps an activity log — the audit trail evaluators expect internally even when it is not a FAR requirement.

5. Solution & submit

Technical Solutioning produces a grounded architecture mapped to requirements. Workspace exports a styled Marln proposal DOCX. Pricing and calendar keep cost volumes and deadlines on the same operating picture.
