We do not sell or share your personal information.

MARLN Corporation does not sell personal information about our customers, website visitors or anyone else, and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months. Your proposals, grants and content belong to you.

1. Who we are

JunoRFP is provided by MARLN Corporation (“MARLN”, “we”, “us”). For personal information we collect through our website, sales and billing, MARLN is the controller (or “business”). For content our customers put into the JunoRFP application, the customer is the controller and MARLN acts as a processor (or “service provider”) under our subscription agreement and data processing agreement.

2. What we collect

  • Account and contact details: name, work email, organization, job title, country, phone and team size, when you register, request a quote or contact us.
  • Billing information: plan, billing period and invoices. Card details are entered on Stripe’s secure checkout page and are never stored by MARLN.
  • Customer content: solicitations, proposals, grants, budgets, tasks and other material you upload to or create in JunoRFP.
  • Usage and technical data: AI usage, feature usage, device and browser type, IP address and logs needed to run, secure and support the service.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use it, and our legal bases

  • To provide, maintain and support JunoRFP, and to process payments (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and enforce usage limits (legitimate interests).
  • To meet tax, accounting and legal obligations (legal obligation).
  • To send product updates and marketing, only where you have agreed or where the law allows, with an unsubscribe link in every message (consent or legitimate interests).
  • To place non-essential cookies, only if you click “Accept all” (consent).

We do not use customer content to train AI models, and we do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.

4. AI processing

AI features send the relevant text to our AI provider (OpenAI) through our secure server to generate a response. Under our provider’s API terms, this data is not used to train their models by default. Each workspace has usage limits and is processed in line with our subscription agreement.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run JunoRFP, under contracts that restrict them to processing it on our instructions:

  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Netlify: website hosting, forms and server functions
  • Amazon Web Services: application hosting in the United States or Canada
  • OpenAI: AI processing

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or acquisition, in which case this statement would continue to apply.

6. International transfers

We host customer workspaces in the United States, or in Canada if you choose. Where personal information is transferred out of the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Saudi Arabia, the UAE or another region with transfer rules, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, and the transfer mechanisms required by local law.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account data is kept for the life of your subscription, then deleted or anonymized within 90 days unless the law requires longer, for example for tax records. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and logged, and our payment provider is certified to PCI DSS Level 1.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict processing; to withdraw consent at any time; and to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising (we do none of these). To make a request, email contact@marlncorp.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by law, generally 30 days (45 days in California). We will not discriminate against you for using your rights. If your data is in a customer’s workspace, we will pass your request to that customer.

Common questions

Does JunoRFP replace our grant database?

For many teams, yes: JunoRFP includes grant search, fit scoring and alerts. If you rely heavily on detailed funder giving history, you can keep that research tool and use JunoRFP for writing, reviews and award management.

Will the AI make things up?

Drafts are built from your approved content and show their sources. When there isn't enough support for a requirement, JunoRFP flags it for an expert instead of inventing an answer, and nothing is exported until an approver signs off.

Is our content used to train AI models?

No. JunoRFP uses OpenAI's business API, which does not train on customer data by default, and MARLN does not train any model on customer content.

Can our data stay in Canada?

Yes. Choose the AWS Canada region during setup to keep your workspace in Canada. US teams are hosted in AWS US regions.

Which privacy laws does JunoRFP support?

JunoRFP's controls are designed to support the CCPA/CPRA and other US state privacy laws, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, and the GDPR for teams that bid internationally. You act as the business or controller, and MARLN acts as your service provider or processor under a data processing agreement.

How long does setup take?

Teams are live on their first real bid within 10 working days.
